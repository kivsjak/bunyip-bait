How to play

Land 5 or more matching symbols touching up, down, left or right to win. Winning symbols drop away and new ones fall in.

Every win leaves burley on its spots. The next win on a burley spot turns it into a ×2 multiplier, then ×3, ×4 and up to ×32. A cluster's win is multiplied by the sum of the multipliers under it.

Bunyip bite: after the drops end, the bunyip may surface on a multiplier spot and swallow every multiplier next to it into one. The board then refills once more.

3, 4, 5 or 6 coolers award 10, 12, 15 or 20 free spins. Free spins start with 3–5 burley spots, and spots and multipliers stay for the whole feature. 3 coolers during free spins add 5 more.

Paytable

Pays are multiples of the bet for a cluster of that many symbols.
